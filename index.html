TMB Trail Companion

A private trail-companion app made for a group of friends who hiked the Tour du Mont Blanc, August 18–28, 2026. The app is now a self-contained archive of that trip.

Privacy policy

This app collects no data. It does not transmit any information off your device. Everything you see — the itinerary, maps, weather briefs, crew roster, and merit pins — is bundled inside the app itself. Anything you enter (such as your display name) is stored only on your device and never sent anywhere. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services.

During the trip (August 2026), an earlier version of this app let crew members voluntarily share their location with each other. That feature is retired; the current app neither requests nor transmits location data.
